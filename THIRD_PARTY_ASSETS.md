# Third-party assets

Assets used by the City / Mall / Office demo (`source/maps/city_demo/`), added to
keep it visually separate from the FireRed/LeafGreen-specific tilesets and sprites
already in this repo (which stay Nintendo/Game Freak IP and are untouched by this demo).

## RPG Urban Pack — Kenney

- **Author:** Kenney (www.kenney.nl)
- **License:** CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain) — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- **Source:** https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-urban-pack
- **Used for:** `assets/sprites/environment/tilesets/city_tileset.png` — sidewalk, road,
  building roofs, entrance door, trees, and lamp posts in the City scene.
- Attribution is not required by the license; credited here anyway.

## Roguelike/RPG Pack — Kenney

- **Author:** Kenney Vleugels for Kenney (www.kenney.nl), with help by Lynn Evers
- **License:** CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain) — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- **Source:** https://kenney.nl/assets/roguelike-rpg-pack
- **Used for:** `assets/sprites/environment/tilesets/interior_tileset.png` — floor
  tiles, shop counters/awnings/shelving, benches, plants, office desks/chairs,
  filing cabinets, bookshelves, reception desk, and doors in the Mall and Office scenes.
- Attribution is not required by the license; credited here anyway.

## Player sprite (unchanged, not third-party for this addition)

The player spritesheet (`assets/sprites/people/red/red_normal.png` and its
LeafGreen counterpart) is untouched — the City/Mall/Office demo reuses
`source/player/player.tscn` as-is.
